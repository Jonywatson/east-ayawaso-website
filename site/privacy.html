Privacy notice

[Draft for review by the office and its legal adviser]

This notice explains how the Office of Hon. Ahmed Baba Jamal, Member of Parliament for East Ayawaso, handles personal information you send through this website, in line with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

What we collect

When you use the contact form, we collect your name, your phone number and email if you give them, your community, and your message.

Why we collect it

We use your details only to read and respond to your message, and to follow up on the issue you raise. We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated purposes.

Who can see it

Messages are delivered to the MP's office and are read only by authorised staff. Our form provider processes messages on our behalf in order to deliver them.

How long we keep it

We keep messages for [period to be confirmed by the office], then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected, or have it deleted. Contact the office at [email to be supplied].

Photos

Photos on this site are published with the permission of the office. If you appear in a photo and would like it removed, please contact the office.
